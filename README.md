# 🚗 Indian Vehicle Registration & Market Opportunity Analysis (2020–2025)

## 📌 Project Overview

The Indian automobile industry is constantly evolving, with changing vehicle preferences, manufacturer performance and fuel technologies.

This project analyses Indian vehicle registration data from 2020 to 2025 using Tableau to explore market trends, geographical distribution, manufacturer performance and changes in vehicle technology.

Rather than focusing only on registration volumes, this project aims to identify meaningful market patterns and explore how automobile companies can use registration insights to understand market performance and identify potential growth opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objective is to transform vehicle registration data into meaningful business insights that can support automobile market research and decision-making.

Key business questions explored:

- Which vehicle categories dominate Indian vehicle registrations?
- Which states have the highest vehicle registration volumes?
- Do states with the largest market size also experience the highest growth?
- Are the leading manufacturers also the fastest-growing manufacturers?
- Which vehicle models and manufacturers contribute most to registrations?
- How are fuel preferences and electric vehicle registrations changing?
- What opportunities can be explored across different automobile market segments?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau:** Data visualisation, calculated fields, table calculations and interactive dashboards.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Initial data inspection and data preparation.
- **Kaggle:** Dataset source.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The project uses an Indian vehicle registration dataset covering 2020–2025.

Important attributes include:

- Registration Year
- Registration Month
- State
- Maker Name
- Vehicle Model
- Vehicle Category
- Fuel Type
- Vehicle Count
- Pollution Norm

The `Vehicle Count` field is used as the primary measure for calculating total vehicle registrations.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

The analysis revealed several interesting patterns:

**1. Two-Wheelers Dominate Vehicle Registrations**

Two-wheelers account for a significant share of total vehicle registrations, highlighting their importance in the Indian automobile market.

**2. Uttar Pradesh Leads in Total Registrations**

Uttar Pradesh recorded the highest vehicle registration volume in the analysis. Its large population may be one contributing factor, although further analysis is required to establish the relationship.

**3. Market Size Does Not Always Mean Higher Growth**

The Market Size vs. Market Growth analysis revealed that states with the highest total registrations were not necessarily the states with the highest registration growth in the latest year.

This highlights the importance of evaluating both existing market size and recent growth when exploring potential market opportunities.

**4. Leading Manufacturers Are Not Always Growth Leaders**

Manufacturers with the highest overall registration volumes were not necessarily the manufacturers experiencing the highest registration growth in the latest year.

This comparison provides a broader perspective on manufacturer performance.

**5. Fuel and Technology Trends**

The analysis explores fuel-type distribution, electric vehicle registrations and pollution norm categories to understand the composition of the Indian vehicle market.
